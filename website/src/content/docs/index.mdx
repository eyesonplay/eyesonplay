## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Watches the video" icon="seti:video">
    Point it at a live stream (HLS, RTMP), a video URL or an upload. It finds the players and the ball in every
    frame, tracks them, and maps them onto the pitch or court as the camera pans.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Calls the events" icon="list-format">
    Football: possession, passes, shots, ball out, corners. Tennis: serves, hits, bounces in or out, faults and who
    won the point. Every event is JSON with a confidence.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shows it live" icon="laptop">
    The dashboard plays the video with detections drawn on it, next to an animated TV-style mini pitch or court,
    with a live event feed, all over WebSocket.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Feeds your systems" icon="rocket">
    An API-key protected feed streams matches and events to betting, media or club platforms in real time.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How it works

<div class="pipeline">
Video ─▶ FFmpeg ─▶ detection (YOLO, TrackNet) ─▶ ball + player tracking ─▶ pitch/court calibration
     ─▶ event engine ─▶ Redis ─▶ API + WebSocket ─▶ dashboard and integration feed
</div>

One camera, no stadium installation. Calibration comes from the lines on the pitch or court, so it works on
ordinary broadcast footage. Nothing is invented: when the camera shows a close-up, positions are marked
unknown instead of guessed. [Read more](guides/how-it-works/).
